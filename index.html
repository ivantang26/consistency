A simple percentage. A clearer picture.

A consistency rule measures how much of your profit came from your single best trading day. A smaller percentage means your profit is spread across more days.

Best day profit÷Profit in the window×100

Start with the right window

For an evaluation, this is usually the evaluation so far. For a payout, it may be the current payout cycle. A carried balance is not necessarily profit in your new window. The plan details in the calculator explain the presets.

Keep losing days in the calculation

For a net-profit rule, losses reduce your total profit but leave your best day intact. Your percentage can exceed 100%. FTMO’s legacy Best Day rule is different: it counts only profitable days. Choose the matching preset.

Find the profit you still need

Divide your best day by the rule as a decimal, then subtract your current profit. We round the required amount upward to the first qualifying cent. If an evaluation has a higher profit target, the remaining amount includes that target too.

Find the least trading days

Divide 100 by your consistency percentage and round up. A 35% rule needs at least 3 profitable days from a fresh window. A firm can require more: Tradeify Growth needs 5 qualifying wins, using the profit threshold for your account size. Enter completed counts or use the journal to see how many days remain. The earliest-finish estimate also accounts for remaining profit while keeping future days at or below your current best day.

A few good questions

Does a big day fail my account?

The featured percentage rules generally require more profit before passing or requesting a payout. Consistency is separate from drawdown and other account breach rules.

Does account size change the percentage?

Usually the percentage belongs to the plan. Account size can change the evaluation profit target, which changes the best-day amount you can have at that target.

What if I make more on my best day?

It raises that day’s total. Treat the scenario planner as a new trading day. Extra profit on the same day can increase your best day and the total profit you need.

Why can 35.00% still be above 35%?

The displayed percentage is rounded to two decimals. Compliance is calculated from the original amounts, so a value such as 35.001% still exceeds 35%.

Where are my numbers stored?

In this browser’s local storage on this device. Export a summary to keep a separate copy. There is no account, cloud sync, or connection to your broker.
