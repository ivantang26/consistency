## Apex

[EOD payout policy](https://apextraderfunding.com/help-center/eod-trailing-drawdown-accounts/eod-payouts/) and [Intraday payout policy](https://apextraderfunding.com/help-center/uncategorized/intraday-trailing-drawdown-payouts/) provide the sizes, inclusive winning-day thresholds, and below-50% payout requirement. Each calculation window starts at account inception or the last approved payout. Legacy plans are deliberately excluded. Safety nets, minimum balances, payout amounts, and account lifecycle restrictions remain outside the calculation.

The [general consistency article](https://apextraderfunding.com/help-center/additional-helpful-items/50-consistency-requirement/) contradicts itself: its prose requires below 50%, but an example marks exactly 50% as met. The presets follow the stricter wording of the payout policies and disclose that choice in the account notes. An exactly 50% result therefore needs one additional cent of net profit under this interpretation; the firm dashboard remains the account-specific reference.

## Take Profit Trader

[Rule 5](https://takeprofittraderhelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15170316538013-Rule-5-Be-Consistent) requires below 50% and three days with at least one trade. [Rule 1](https://takeprofittraderhelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15169070804125-Rule-1-Hit-Your-Profit-Target) supplies the five account targets. These are Test presets, not funded PRO or PRO+ presets.

## Earn2Trade

[Maintain Consistency](https://help.earn2trade.com/en/articles/3849975-what-is-the-maintain-consistency-rule) applies to both evaluations and excludes LiveSim/Live stages. The preset accepts equality using the main rule's wording that no day may exceed 30%; the examples recommend staying below that percentage. [Gauntlet Mini](https://www.earn2trade.com/gauntlet-mini) and [Trader Career Path](https://www.earn2trade.com/trader-career-path) list current sizes and no separate day minimum. Gauntlet target values were additionally checked against the public account-tier data loaded by that official product page, including its $11,000 GAU200 target. Career progression after evaluation is outside the preset.

## Alpha Futures

[Consistency guidance](https://help.alpha-futures.com/en/articles/9492048-consistency-rule) provides the evaluation percentages and account-target tables. [Terms, Schedule 1](https://alpha-futures.com/terms-and-conditions) confirms the two-day minimum and 6%/8% targets. These presets allow equality as the evaluation tables do; Qualified-stage percentages and payout windows differ and are not represented by these presets.
